# Pixel Dash - Endless Runner

Pixel Dash is a 2D endless parkour platformer game set in a vibrant pixel world. Test your reflexes and see how long you can survive in this fast-paced, action-packed adventure!

## 🎮 Play the Game! 🎮

You can play Pixel Dash directly in your browser! Click here to start playing.
https://shanmukha.itch.io/pixel-dash


## Description

In Pixel Dash, you take on the role of a nimble character dashing through a procedurally generated world of platforms. The goal is simple: survive for as long as you can. But don't be fooled by the simplicity, as the game's speed increases over time, and you'll have to navigate through various traps and obstacles.

## How to Play

The controls are easy to learn, but hard to master:

* **Jump / Double Jump:** `[Spacebar]`
* **Slide:** `[Left Shift]`

## Features

* **Endless Action:** Classic endless runner gameplay that is easy to pick up but offers a high level of challenge.
* **Deep Customization:** Unlock a variety of color combinations for your character and the platforms to personalize your experience.
* **Dynamic Day and Night Cycle:** Experience the world of Pixel Dash in a dynamic lighting environment.
* **Procedurally Generated Levels:** Every run is a new challenge with procedurally generated platforms and obstacles.
* **Stunning Pixel Art:** Immerse yourself in the game's beautiful pixel art style and atmospheric parallax scrolling backgrounds.
* **Compete with Yourself:** Beat your own high score and strive for a new personal best with every run.


* **Game Engine:** Unity
* **Programming Language:** C#
